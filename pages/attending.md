### Registration

Registration is through AAAI and covers one symposium for all three days, the electronic proceedings, coffee breaks, and Thursday's opening reception.

<p><a class="btn btn-brand" href="https://aaai.getregistered.net/2026-fall-symposium/register"><i class="fa-solid fa-calendar-check" aria-hidden="true"></i> Register</a></p>

- **Fees** (late rate, after October 2): $495 for AAAI members, $660 for nonmembers, $325 for student members, and $435 for nonmember students. AAAI also offers registration bundled with membership. See the [AAAI symposium page](https://aaai.org/conference/fall-symposia/2026-fall-symposium-series-2/) for all options.
- **Desk hours:** Thursday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, Friday 8:30 AM–5:00 PM, Saturday 8:30–11:00 AM, in the second-floor pre-function area outside Fitzgerald Ballroom C.
- **Visa letters:** available to registered attendees and accepted authors through the form linked in your AAAI registration confirmation.
- **Questions about registration:** fssreg@aaai.org.

### Getting there

- **By Metro:** the Westin is a short walk from the Ballston-MU station (Orange and Silver lines). From Reagan National Airport (DCA), take the Blue line and change at Rosslyn. From Dulles (IAD), the Silver line runs directly to Ballston-MU.
- **By car:** self-parking and valet at the hotel are $40 per day each.
- **Hotel:** the AAAI group rate closed on October 21, but rooms at the Westin may still be available at the hotel's regular rate. [Book a room at the Westin](https://www.marriott.com/event-reservations/reservation-link.mi?id=1776205479949&key=GRP&app=resvlink).

### Food nearby

AAAI provides drinks and light snacks at the coffee breaks, and a reception on Thursday evening. Breakfast and lunch are on your own, and lunch runs 12:30–2:00 on Thursday and Friday.

**Lunch together.** We'd like lunch to be a chance to meet people, so the organizers plan to announce a spot each day that anyone can join. For sit-down group lunches, AAAI staff suggest Ted's Bulletin, The Salt Line, or Silver Diner.

Everything below is in Ballston, within roughly a 10-minute walk of the hotel, and each list runs from nearest to farthest. 🏨 marks places inside the hotel, 🌱 vegan and vegetarian options, and 🚫🌾 gluten-free options. Distances and walking times are from the Westin and are estimates. Hours and menus change, so check before you go, and ask the restaurant directly about allergies.
